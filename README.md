Entry for [One Star, Written in Sap](https://make.fun/bounty/u-steady-puffin-78-1922-one-star-written-in-sap-2ea1f4f4) on make.fun.

`main.py` writes a one-line 1-star review to `/output/entry.txt`. The reviewer
is a monstera, furious that its owner dragged its pot six inches from the
window for "better energy", and it has spent three months photosynthesizing
purely out of spite. The turn lands in the final clause: the review is written
in sap, because the owner took its pen too.

Run: `python3 main.py` — writes `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 216 characters,
inside the 1–279 gate).
